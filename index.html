What does the Civic RS cost in 2026?

Honda Atlas Cars lists the Civic RS 1.5L at Rs 10,100,000 in Pakistan. The amount appears on the manufacturer's model page and price calculator. It gives buyers a clear starting point, but it does not cover every payment needed to put the car on the road.

The final bill depends on your tax status, registration area, optional equipment and insurance choices. Before paying a booking amount, request a written estimate that separates the vehicle price from every additional charge.

Quick answer: Budget beyond the displayed Rs 1.01 crore figure. Ask the dealership to calculate your exact invoice using your destination and active taxpayer status, then check registration costs with the relevant excise department.

Engine, power and driving experience

Honda's official specification sheet shows a 1,498 cc turbocharged petrol engine producing 176 hp and 220 Nm of torque. The RS uses a continuously variable automatic transmission, often called a CVT. It delivers power without the usual fixed gear changes of a conventional automatic gearbox.

When the driver asks for faster acceleration, the turbo engine provides stronger pulling power. A brief test drive is still the best way to judge throttle response, cabin noise and how the car behaves over uneven roads.

What fuel average should you expect?

A fixed fuel average would be misleading because driving conditions change so much across Pakistan. Heavy traffic in Karachi, short trips across Lahore and open stretches of the Islamabad motorway network place different demands on the engine. Air conditioning, tyre pressure, traffic stops and driving style matter too.

Turbo petrol engines also respond to hard acceleration with higher fuel use. If economy is a major concern, ask a current owner about a route similar to yours and check the vehicle's own consumption display during a longer test drive. Use the fuel grade recommended in Honda's owner manual and follow the maintenance schedule instead of relying on social media mileage claims.

Interior, comfort and exterior equipment

The RS gets a more distinctive look than an ordinary family sedan. Honda lists a black rear spoiler, RS badges, black painted handles, LED headlights, updated taillights and matte black 17 inch alloy wheels.

Inside, the equipment list includes perforated leather seats, a 9 inch Android based touchscreen, wireless charging, an auto dimming rearview mirror, rain sensing wipers and a card key. Honda also lists illuminated vanity mirrors and a driver seat height adjustment. Some interior colours and accessories may cost extra, so check the quotation instead of assuming every display car feature is included.

Sit in the rear seat, check access for older passengers and confirm that your child seat fits the ISOFIX mounting points. The published 409 litre luggage space is useful, but test a stroller or travel bags in person if you carry them often.

Honda Sensing and everyday safety

Honda Pakistan includes Honda Sensing in its Civic RS presentation. This package supports the driver with electronic assistance, but it does not replace attention, safe speed or proper following distance. The exact functions and conditions must be checked against the brochure supplied for the vehicle being delivered.

Ask the dealer to demonstrate the controls before leaving the showroom. On crowded streets or roads with faded lane markings, your own judgement remains essential.

On road costs in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Peshawar

Two buyers can pay different amounts for the same car even when Honda's displayed price is identical. Registration charges depend on the province or authority, while federal advance tax and the buyer's filer status can change the overall calculation. Optional insurance, delivery and dealer selected accessories add further costs.

In Karachi, request a Sindh registration estimate. In Lahore, check the current Punjab Excise schedule. Buyers registering in Islamabad can consult the Islamabad Excise fee calculator. For Peshawar, Abbottabad and other Khyber Pakhtunkhwa locations, confirm the latest rates with the relevant provincial office. Always use the current rates for your registration date, not an old screenshot or a previous year's fee table.

Federal withholding rules also change through annual finance legislation. The Federal Board of Revenue publishes an updated rate card for tax year 2027. Your dealership should show the current amount separately on the booking or payment statement rather than hide it inside a single unexplained total.

What to check before booking

Start with a formal quotation from an authorized Honda dealership. It should show the model, colour, base amount, booking payment, estimated delivery date and all additional taxes and fees. Ask whether the quoted price can change before delivery and what happens if delivery takes longer than planned.

Next, inspect the car you will actually receive. Match its chassis details and model variant to the invoice. Check paint condition, alloy wheels, seat material, multimedia system, lights and supplied accessories.

Finally, request the warranty booklet, service schedule and written explanation of any extended coverage. Use the correct engine oil and follow Honda’s service intervals. A low initial service quote is less useful than knowing the full long term ownership routine.

Should you consider a used Civic RS?

A used RS may cost less initially, but condition matters more than the discount. Ask for service receipts and check whether the mileage matches the condition of the tyres, seats and brakes. Inspect the engine for leaks, listen for unusual noises and test the CVT during gentle and strong acceleration.

Arrange an independent inspection and verify registration documents through the relevant authority before paying. Repair history, accident damage, pending taxes and outstanding finance matter more than a polished advertisement. Compare the total used car cost with the latest new car quote, including immediate repairs and insurance.

Is the RS the right Civic for you?

The flagship version makes sense for someone who wants its stronger performance character, sportier design and added cabin features. The Oriel remains worth considering if the difference in purchase price is better spent on savings, insurance or other family needs.

For the most sensible decision, put the current dealer invoice beside your monthly fuel, tyre, insurance and servicing budget. Drive both variants if available.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Honda Civic RS Turbo price in Pakistan in 2026?

Honda Atlas lists the Civic RS 1.5L Turbo at PKR 10,100,000, checked on 18 October 2026. Registration, applicable taxes, insurance and optional extras can increase the final amount.

Is the Honda Civic RS Turbo available with an automatic transmission?

Yes. The Pakistan specification Civic RS uses a continuously variable automatic transmission, or CVT, with a 1,498 cc turbocharged petrol engine.

What is the engine power of the Civic RS in Pakistan?

How much more does Civic RS cost than Civic Oriel?

What is the on road price of Civic RS in Karachi or Lahore?
